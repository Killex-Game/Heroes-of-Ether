Owner decisions implemented
- Premium multiplies credited absorption kills by two immediately. Natural kills were already multiplied and are not multiplied again. Actual defeated-player achievements remain based on actual defeats. VIP XP multiplication remains after accessory bonuses.
- Clan kill quests now use the actual credited personal kill delta for every source, including Premium absorption. Personal-cap display counters do not become clan progress. Existing exit-only settlement and strict exit-before-deadline rule remain unchanged.
- Booster purchases accept 10 or 100 units, computed and validated on the server, with independent idempotency signatures. Legacy omitted quantity remains 10. Costs, inventory, spending achievements and event purchase counts commit together.
- Trophy discounts now apply to personal skin sets and hats/bows at transaction time. The shop uses the same calculation and receives dated trophy periods; the strongest active discount can expire into a weaker one. Membership departure and exact expiry remove entitlement. All ordinary spending achievements use actual debits. Pending batch purchases use the pending clan state.
- The discount note moved from wardrobe to the shop below its category tabs, with remaining days/hours/minutes/seconds. Its timer only runs while the shop is open; a second does not rebuild the product grid.
- Owner administration can settle the current quest/battle season early and start the other mode until the calendar period end. Revision/season id, request id, reason, ownership and durable audit are required. Same-day quest cycles have distinct receipt keys. No live season is switched by deployment.
- Stone causes five seconds of confused movement and a mild rotating world view, with inverse aim transformation and reduced-motion support. Web anchoring, shields and reflection retain their own rules.
- Clan quest chests notify Inventory/Treasury, not Clan. Clan applications still notify Clan.
- Remove under-timer explanatory copy without changing settlement rules. Status layout, title plaques, compact purchase buttons, wider PC arena chat, full accessory hints and entrance wording updated. Chat strips links, silently rejects URL-only content, keeps failed drafts and retry; technical send/history statuses removed.
- Preview frame squares removed; the portrait retains a circular shaded disc. Six owner-provided original ether purchase PNGs restored byte-for-byte. Ether generator button and success receipt show the currency icon; exchange remains 100000 natural -> 10 wallet ether.
- Easter lobby uses a simple static repeating generated pattern. Black Friday restores the earliest recorded pattern (6fbba37). Arena backgrounds, avatars, crests, title medals, news banners and named appearance themes are unchanged.
