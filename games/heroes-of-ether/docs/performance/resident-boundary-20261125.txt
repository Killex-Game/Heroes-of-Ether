Before consuming inputs across a daily clan-quest boundary, the owner journals
the previous completed state. A position-only prefix receives one empty time
anchor at that boundary, then the food sample, then a post-cutoff watermark.
Ordinary position-only frames still do not write empty journal records. The
boundary path cannot advance physics or publish snapshots/paid ACKs before its
durable prefix. A backwards or multi-window clock jump fences the room.

An unobserved exit during recovery is dated at recovery time, not the last
observed tick: recovering after the cutoff must not manufacture a timely exit.
Explicit durable life-ended events retain their recorded timestamps. This
preserves the owner's current exit-only clan rule; the historical immediate
credit mode remains covered separately.
